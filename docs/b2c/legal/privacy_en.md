JOJO Corporation ("we" or "us") handles the personal information of travelers who book the luggage delivery service "BondEx" through the dedicated booking site ("you") as follows.

## 1. Information we collect
1. Information you enter when booking: your name, email address, phone number (WhatsApp number), pickup and delivery hotels and dates of stay, pickup and delivery dates, number of bags, and the language of the screen.
2. Information we record during the booking: request number, booking status, the version of the terms you agreed to and when, the name of the travel company that referred you, the carrier's tracking number and delivery status, and records of charges and refunds.
3. Card information: your card number, expiry date and security code are collected directly by our payment provider (Stripe). We do not collect or store your card number or security code. We receive only the card brand, the last 4 digits and an identifier issued by the payment provider.
4. Access records: to prevent fraud and investigate problems, we may record the date and time of access, IP address, browser type and similar information. The private links (URLs) for your booking page and similar pages are not sent to analytics tools.

## 2. How we use it
1. To accept, confirm, change and cancel bookings, check with hotels, request pickup from the carrier and arrange delivery
2. To charge and refund, and to contact you if a payment fails
3. To contact you about your booking, pickup and delivery (by email; by phone or WhatsApp if urgent)
4. To answer questions and investigate damage or loss
5. To prevent misuse, keep the Service secure and investigate problems
6. To comply with the law
7. To produce statistics that do not identify individuals and to improve the Service

## 3. Disclosure to third parties
We do not disclose your personal information to third parties except as follows.
1. To the carrier (Sagawa Express Co., Ltd.), to the extent needed for transport: your name, the names and addresses of the pickup and delivery hotels, the number of bags, and the pickup and delivery dates.
2. To the pickup and delivery hotels, to the extent needed to hold and receive your bags: your name, request number, number of bags and dates.
3. To the travel company that referred you, we may share whether you have booked and the status of the booking. [To confirm, L8: what is shared with the referring travel company is set by contract]
4. Where required or permitted by law, including the Act on the Protection of Personal Information.
