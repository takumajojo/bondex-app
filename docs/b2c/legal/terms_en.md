Effective date: ●●, 2026

These terms apply when a traveler ("you") books the BondEx luggage delivery service (the "Service") provided by JOJO Corporation (株式会社JOJO, "we", "us") through our dedicated booking site. By booking, you agree to these terms.

## 1. The Service
1. We arrange the delivery of your luggage from the hotel in Japan where you are staying (the "pickup hotel") to the next hotel in Japan where you will stay (the "delivery hotel").
2. The luggage is carried by our courier partner, Sagawa Express Co., Ltd. (the "courier"). We take your booking, confirm with the hotels, request the pickup, collect the fee and handle your inquiries. [Check L1]
3. The Service can only be booked through the dedicated booking site provided by our partner (Hankyu Travel).

## 2. Booking and contract
1. To book, you enter the pickup hotel, delivery hotel, stay dates, pickup date, delivery date, number of bags, your name, email address and WhatsApp number, agree to these terms and register a credit card.
2. Your booking is confirmed — and the contract is formed — when we have registered your card and confirmed that both the pickup hotel and the delivery hotel can hold your luggage. We notify you by email.
3. If we need to check with a hotel, your booking stays "pending" until we have done so. Please do not leave your luggage at the hotel while it is pending. If we cannot confirm by the deadline, or a hotel cannot hold your luggage, we cancel the booking at no charge.
4. Bookings must be made by 18:30 Japan time on the day before pickup. Card registration must be completed by 18:00 that day. If the card entry screen is left open for 30 minutes, the request is cancelled.
5. Up to 10 bags can be booked in one booking.

## 3. Price
1. The price is 4,980 JPY per bag excluding tax (5,478 JPY including tax). Between Okinawa and other prefectures, it is 13,000 JPY per bag excluding tax (14,300 JPY including tax).
2. Consumption tax is 10% of the total before tax (rounded down to the yen).
3. The price shown at booking does not change after confirmation, except as set out in section 5.

## 4. Payment
1. Payment is by credit card only (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, American Express, Diners Club, Discover). UnionPay is not accepted.
2. Nothing is charged at booking; your card is only registered.
3. We charge your registered card after we have confirmed that the courier has picked up your luggage. By booking, you agree to be charged later in this way. [Check L7]
4. Card details are handled by our payment provider (Stripe). We do not store your card number.
5. If the payment with your registered card fails, we email you a link to a payment page, where you confirm the payment or pay with another card. If payment is not made: [Check L9]

## 5. Charging for the number of bags actually picked up
1. The fee is based on the number of bags the courier actually received.
2. If you hand over more bags than booked, the additional bags are charged at the same price per bag as in section 3. If you hand over fewer, you are charged for the actual number.
3. If the number differs from your booking, we check the courier's records before finalizing the amount.
4. If a difference is found after charging, we charge the additional amount or refund the difference.

## 6. Handing over your luggage
1. Please leave your luggage at the front desk of the pickup hotel by 11:00 on the pickup date. The courier brings the shipping labels.
2. If the courier cannot pick up your luggage because it was not handed over in time: [Decide D1]
3. You are responsible for packing and locking your luggage so that it can be transported safely. We recommend a protective cover for suitcases (without one, scratches to the outer surface may not be covered).
4. The size and weight of each bag must be within the courier's limits. [Decide D3]

## 7. Items you must not send
Do not put the following in your luggage. The courier may refuse such luggage, and damage to or loss of these items is not covered.
- Cash, securities, precious metals, jewelry, passports and other valuables
- Fragile items and precision equipment (such as laptops)
- Dangerous or flammable goods and items whose transport is prohibited by law
- Perishable goods
- Other items the courier does not accept under its terms of carriage [Decide D4]

## 8. Delivery
1. We arrange delivery to the delivery hotel on the delivery date you selected.
2. Delivery may be delayed by weather, traffic, disasters or other circumstances. [Decide D2]
3. Storage after the delivery hotel receives your luggage follows the hotel's rules.

## 9. Cancellation
Cancellation is governed by our separate Cancellation Policy.

## 10. Damage or loss

2. If you notice damage or loss, please contact us promptly after receiving your luggage. [Check L3]
