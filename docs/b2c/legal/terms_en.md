These Terms set out the conditions under which travelers ("you") book the luggage delivery service "BondEx" (the "Service") provided by JOJO Corporation ("we" or "us") through the dedicated booking site. By booking, you agree to these Terms. All times are Japan time.

## Article 1 (The Service)
1. The Service arranges the delivery of your luggage from a hotel in Japan where you are staying (the "pickup hotel") to the next hotel in Japan where you will stay (the "delivery hotel").
2. Your luggage is carried by the carrier we engage (Sagawa Express Co., Ltd., the "carrier"). We accept bookings, check with the hotels, request the pickup from the carrier, collect payment and answer your questions. [L1]
3. The Service can only be booked through the dedicated booking site provided to you by our partner.

## Article 2 (Booking and contract)
1. To book, you enter the pickup hotel, delivery hotel, dates of stay, pickup date, delivery date, number of bags, your name, email address and phone number (WhatsApp) on the booking site, agree to these Terms and register a credit card.
2. Your booking is confirmed, and the contract is formed, when we have confirmed your card registration and that both the pickup hotel and the delivery hotel can accept your luggage. We will email you the confirmation.
3. If we need to check with a hotel, your booking stays "being checked" until we can confirm. Do not leave your luggage at the hotel while it is being checked. If we cannot confirm by the deadline, or a hotel cannot accept your luggage, we will cancel the booking at no charge.
4. The booking deadline is [D0: 17:00 or 18:30] on the day before pickup (the "booking deadline"). Please complete card registration at least 30 minutes before the booking deadline. If the card entry screen is left open for 30 minutes, the request is cancelled.
5. You can book up to 10 bags per booking.

## Article 3 (Price)
1. The price is 4,980 JPY per bag excluding tax (5,478 JPY including tax). Between Okinawa and any other prefecture, it is 13,000 JPY per bag excluding tax (14,300 JPY including tax).
2. Consumption tax is 10% of the total excluding tax (fractions of 1 yen are rounded down).
3. The price per bag shown at booking does not change after confirmation. The amount charged is calculated on the actual number of bags under Article 5.

## Article 4 (Payment)
1. Payment is by credit card only (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, American Express, Diners Club, Discover). UnionPay is not accepted.
2. At booking, your card is only registered. No charge is made.
3. We charge your registered card after we confirm that the carrier has picked up your luggage. By booking, you agree to be charged later in this way, and that the amount is based on the actual number of bags under Article 5. [L7]
4. Card details are handled by our payment provider (Stripe). We do not store your card number.
5. If the payment on your registered card fails, we will email you a link to a payment page (when it fails, and again 1, 3 and 7 days later). You agree to complete the payment by authenticating or using another card as instructed. Handling of unpaid amounts: [L9]

## Article 5 (Charging for the bags actually picked up)
1. The price is calculated on the number of bags the carrier actually received.
2. If you hand over more bags than booked, each extra bag is charged at the same price per bag as in Article 3. If you hand over fewer, you are charged for the actual number.
3. If the actual number differs from the booking, we confirm it against the carrier's records before fixing the amount.
4. If, after charging, the number turns out to be different, we charge the extra amount or refund the difference.

## Article 6 (Handing over your luggage)
1. Please leave your luggage at the front desk of the pickup hotel by 11:00 on the pickup date. The carrier brings the shipping labels.
2. If your luggage is not handed over and the carrier cannot pick it up, we do not charge you. We will check the situation and contact you. If a new pickup is then arranged and the carrier picks up your luggage, you will be charged under Articles 4 and 5.
3. You are responsible for packing and locking your luggage so that it can withstand transport. We recommend a protective cover for suitcases (without one, scratches to the outside of a suitcase may not be covered).
4. Each bag must be no more than 160 cm in total length, width and height, and no more than 30 kg. For larger or heavier bags, please contact us before booking. We do not charge for any bag the carrier does not accept. [D3: to be confirmed with the carrier]

## Article 7 (Items that cannot be sent and items with limited compensation)
1. The carrier does not accept the following items, so do not pack them (based on the carrier's terms of carriage and guidance):
   - cash, cheques, bills of exchange, share certificates and other securities;
   - credit cards, bank cards and other cards;
   - passports, exam admission tickets, vehicle inspection certificates, tickets and other documents that are difficult to reissue;
   - irreplaceable manuscripts, drawings, tapes, films and recording media;
   - animals; human remains, memorial tablets and Buddhist altars; firearms and swords; poisons and toxic substances;
   - explosives and other dangerous goods (batteries and similar items follow the carrier's rules), and items that may damage other luggage;
   - any item worth more than 300,000 JPY (including tax) per bag;
   - items containing multiple people's personal information;
   - letters (correspondence), items whose transport, movement or distribution is prohibited by law, and items contrary to law or public order and morals.
2. If your luggage contains any of the items above, the carrier may refuse to accept it, and loss of or damage to those items is not covered. [L3]
3. Compensation may be limited or excluded in the following cases:
   - fragile items, or items that easily deteriorate or spoil, where the carrier was not informed that special handling was needed;
   - damage caused by defects or natural wear of the luggage, discoloration or rust due to its nature, natural disasters, unforeseeable abnormal traffic disruption, and similar causes;
   - scratches to the outside of a suitcase without a protective cover.
4. Please keep valuables and fragile items with you rather than packing them.

## Article 8 (Delivery)

2. Delivery may be delayed by weather, traffic, disasters or other circumstances. Refunds for delays are decided case by case, based on the cause of the delay and the actual loss. This paragraph does not limit our liability or any obligation to refund under applicable law. [L2]
3. Storage after the delivery hotel receives your luggage follows the hotel's rules.
